Disaster Recovery Plan

Purpose

This plan helps the organisation recover IT services after an unexpected disruption.

Ransomware or Cyberattack

  • Report the incident immediately to IT.
  • Follow the instructions of the incident lead.
  • Identify affected systems and accounts.
  • Protect evidence and relevant system records.
  • Restore systems from verified clean backups.
  • Monitor systems after recovery.

Hardware Failure

Record the fault, assess its impact and use approved replacement equipment where available. Test the system before returning it to users.

Data Loss

Identify the affected information and locate an appropriate backup. Restore the data safely and check that it is complete.

Network or Internet Failure

Check network equipment, cables, power and provider status. Contact the service provider if necessary and test the network after repairs.

Power Failure or Physical Disaster

Follow building safety and evacuation procedures first. IT systems should only be restored when it is safe to do so.

Backups and Testing

Important information must be backed up regularly. Backups should be protected and tested to confirm that information can be restored.

Review

The IT Manager should coordinate recovery planning and review the plan after incidents, tests and major system changes.
