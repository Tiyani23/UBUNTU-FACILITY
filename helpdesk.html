Service Desk Procedures

Reporting an IT Problem

Employees should report IT problems through the approved service desk email, ticket portal or telephone extension.

Ticket Procedure

  1. Record the Problem: Record the user's details, device and description of the issue.
  2. Classify the Problem: Identify whether the problem concerns hardware, software, accounts, networks or security.
  3. Set Priority: Classify the issue according to its urgency and impact.
  4. Acknowledge the Request: Inform the user that the request has been received.
  5. Troubleshoot: Follow approved steps to identify and resolve the problem.
  6. Escalate: Refer serious or unresolved issues to the IT Manager or relevant technical staff.
  7. Update the User: Inform the user about progress.
  8. Close the Ticket: Record the solution and confirm that the problem is resolved.

Security Incidents

Suspected security incidents must be reported immediately. Employees should not delete suspicious files or interfere with evidence.
