Acceptable Use Policy

Purpose

This policy applies to employees, interns, contractors and approved third parties who use the Centre's IT resources.

Acceptable Use Rules

  • Use IT resources mainly for authorised work.
  • Personal use must be limited and must not disrupt work.
  • Do not access or share unlawful or inappropriate content.
  • Do not bypass security controls.
  • Never share passwords or authentication codes.
  • Be careful when opening suspicious emails and links.
  • Do not connect unknown USB devices to organisation computers.
  • Do not copy confidential information to personal devices.
  • Use approved software and follow licence rules.
  • Keep computers and other devices secure.
  • Report suspected misuse to IT or a manager.

Policy Violations

Employees who violate this policy may have their access suspended and may face disciplinary action.

Employee Responsibilities

All users must read and acknowledge the policy before receiving access to the organisation's IT resources.
