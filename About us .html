Who We Are

Ubuntu Community Health Centre (UCHC) is a healthcare organisation based in Durban, South Africa. Our website provides information about our IT policies and procedures.

Our Mission

To support quality healthcare services through responsible use of information technology and effective management of IT resources.

Our Vision

To promote reliable and secure technology that supports healthcare services and the community.

Our IT Department

The IT department supports computer systems, manages information security, assists users with technical problems and helps maintain reliable technology services.

Our Policies and Procedures

Our standard operating procedures guide staff on how to use IT resources, protect information, report technical problems and respond to system failures.
