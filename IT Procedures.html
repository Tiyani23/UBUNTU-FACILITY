IT Acquisition Procedures

Purpose

These procedures ensure that IT equipment, software and services are suitable, secure and approved.

Acquisition Steps

  1. Request: The employee explains what is needed and why.
  2. Approval: The department head confirms the need and budget.
  3. Technical Assessment: IT checks compatibility, security and support requirements.
  4. Supplier Review: Procurement checks suppliers, prices and warranties.
  5. Purchase Approval: The authorised manager approves the purchase.
  6. Purchase Order: Procurement issues the authorised purchase order.
  7. Delivery: IT checks the equipment or software received.
  8. Asset Registration: IT records the serial number, assigned user, location and warranty.
  9. Installation: IT installs and configures the equipment or software.

Emergency Purchases

Emergency purchases must include a recorded reason and approval from an authorised manager.
